Request account and data deletion

To request deletion of your Powerlifting Performance Hub cloud account, email contact@powerlifting-calculator.com from the email address associated with your Hub account.

Write “Performance Hub account deletion” in the subject. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or backup files. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request.

The request covers your Hub cloud account, sign-in sessions and stored cloud profile, goals, meet plan, results, preferences and history. We will confirm the outcome by email. If some records must be retained, we will explain which records, the reason and the retention period.

Deleting the cloud account does not erase copies stored on your devices or exported backup files. To remove a device copy, open Settings → Device data controls → Reset app data. Delete your exported files separately. Device reset alone does not delete your cloud account.

You can request deletion here even after uninstalling the app.
