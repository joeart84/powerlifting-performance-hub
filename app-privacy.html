Powerlifting Performance Hub privacy

Updated 6 October 2026. Contact: contact@powerlifting-calculator.com.

On your device

Your athlete name, sex, bodyweight, age or optional date of birth, goals, competition history, attempt plan, results and preferences are stored on this device. The app works without an account. Exported backups contain athlete data, but exclude sign-in credentials. You choose where to save or share each exported file.

Optional cloud account

If you sign in, the account service on powerlifting-calculator.com processes your email address and sign-in session. Email codes are used to verify access to your email address. On the web, Google sign-in sends a Google identity credential to this same account service for verification. This Android test version uses email-code sign-in.

Uploading your device data or downloading a cloud copy requires an explicit action. Signing in alone does not upload your athlete profile. Uploaded cloud profiles include athlete information, plans, results, history, goals and preferences. You can request deletion of your account and cloud data through the account deletion page.

Public data and location

Searching OpenPowerlifting athletes, competition listings or performance references sends the search or request to powerlifting-calculator.com. OpenPowerlifting history is public source data. Nearby competition search can use a city and country you enter or your location if you explicitly grant location permission. Location is optional. If your location is saved in preferences and you upload your profile, those preferences are included in the cloud copy.

Android advertisements

The Android test version includes Google AdMob and requests test advertisements only. Ads are hidden during meet-day use and account entry. Google's SDK can process identifiers, device information and advertising interactions; consent availability and ad requests are controlled using Google's consent service. Where required, ad privacy options are available in Settings. We do not send your athlete profile to AdMob.

For Google's practices, see Google Privacy Policy. Production advertisements require a separately reviewed release.

Your choices

You can use the app without signing in, decline optional location access, export your data, sign out, request cloud deletion, or reset device data in Settings. Cloud deletion does not delete exported files or copies on other devices; device reset alone does not delete a cloud account.

Network services may process connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. For retention questions or data-access requests, contact us using the address above. See also the main website privacy policy.
